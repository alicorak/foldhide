Foldhide

A real leather bi-fold wallet for iPhone.

Open your iPhone Duo and it becomes a bi-fold wallet: brushed-metal cards in leather slots, a folded note, a pen and a receipt. On other iPhones the wallet opens straight away.

How to use

  • Tap a card to pull it out of its slot. Tap it again to turn it over.
  • Tap the leather to put it back.
  • Tap the receipt to read it, tap the banknote to unfold it.
  • Drag a card to the other side to rearrange your wallet.
