Foldhide — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

Foldhide is a wallet app for iPhone Duo. This policy explains what the app does with your information: nothing.

Data we collect

None. Foldhide does not collect, store, transmit or sell any personal data. The app has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party SDKs.

Network access

Foldhide does not connect to the internet.

Data stored on your device

Foldhide saves only your preferences (for example the leather colour) in the app's own storage on your device. This information never leaves your device and is deleted when you remove the app.

Device motion

Foldhide reads the device's orientation to move light across the metal cards. This data is used instantly on the device and is never recorded.

Children

Foldhide does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new date.
